Privacy Policy

Knot Stack: Untangle Rope (Düğüm Çöz) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Knot Stack: Untangle Rope (Turkish name: Düğüm Çöz: İp Bulmacası, package com.ereniridere.dugumcoz, "the Game") handles. The Game is developed by Eren İridere, an independent developer ("I", "me").

1. Short version

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. I do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (current level, stars and settings) is stored only on your phone. I do not have a server and I never receive it.
  • The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following on your device so you can continue where you left off: the next level to play, stars earned on each level, language choice, and sound and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

The Game asks for the vibration permission only to give haptic feedback. It can be turned off in the settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded ads (watched voluntarily, for example to get an extra life or hint) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads between levels). These are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.

To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country),
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped), and diagnostic and crash information from the SDK.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. For details see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before personalised ads are shown, the Game displays a consent form provided by Google. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads or decline, and you can change your choice later from the Game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Ads will then be less personalised.

4. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited, non-identifying technical data (such as device model and performance information) to keep the engine working. See the Unity Player Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me and I will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and selling

I do not sell your personal information and I do not share it with anyone other than the advertising provider described above.

7. Security and retention

Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because I do not hold any personal data on a server, most requests can be fulfilled by resetting your advertising ID or uninstalling the Game. For data processed by Google, you can use the tools in your Google account. You can also contact me at any time.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
